  • 01

    line-studio

    live open →

    A cooking mode for the stove. Scales ingredients off a servings stepper, walks the recipe step by step, runs concurrent timers, plans the week and rolls it all into an aggregated shopping list. Single HTML file, no build, no server — recipes load as JSON you drop on the window. Ships with a demo recipe.

    • vanilla-js
    • single-file
    • offline
    • localstorage
  • 02

    video-store

    live open →

    A film diary shelved like a rental store. Every title watched gets a case — VHS, DVD or Blu-ray, each its own size and finish — racked spine-out on a wooden shelf. Pull one down and it turns face-front with the cover art, a date stamp and your rating. Spine colours are sampled from the poster itself.

    • vanilla-js
    • es-modules
    • css-3d
    • canvas
  • 03

    protoboard-studio

    live open →

    A 2D protoboard and stripboard layout designer for planning hand-wired builds. Place breakout boards from a parts library, run jumpers on the front or the back, see through to the other side, let it trace the nets and flag unconnected pins, then export a cut list to work from at the bench. Desktop layout.

    • vanilla-js
    • svg
    • no-build
    • offline
  • 04

    choicer-party

    wip open →

    A browser dubbing booth for Choicer Voicer packages. Drop a package folder or zip, get the scene frame, the subtitle and the original waveform for each line, record over it with a count-in, nudge the timing, then play the dub back over the clip (Firefox) or its frames (everywhere else) with your voices mixed in. First slice of a multiplayer version where each player takes a character.

    • vanilla-js
    • web-audio
    • audioworklet
    • no-build
